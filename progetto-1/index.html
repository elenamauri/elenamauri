Interactive Mattress Configurator

To optimize the lead generation process, I designed a conditional-logic configurator that simplifies complex product variables. The tool successfully automated the quoting process, reducing management time and ensuring that every request is fully profiled and consistent. By delivering a guided, high-value experience, the interface allows users to perceive the product's quality immediately, while enabling the internal team to focus on high-value tasks.

The Challenge

Personalization and cognitive load

Choosing the right mattress is a high-friction decision often plagued by choice paralysis. The objective was to transform a complex set of technical variables into a seamless journey that guides the user toward the perfect product while speaking the brand's unique language.

The Approach

Making technical choices easier

I focused on reducing the user's mental effort. I mapped the entire experience using conditional logic, ensuring that the flow remains relevant and personalized. Every question is a direct result of the previous selection, creating a tailored path that feels like a consultation rather than a form.

Bridging Brand & Functionality

The configurator isn't just a functional tool; it’s a digital extension of the Big Ape identity. Brand Voice: I designed an interface that speaks the same language as the brand—balancing essentiality with engagement. Intuitive Experience: Every visual detail was curated to ensure the experience is as bold and straightforward as the brand itself, maintaining a perfect equilibrium between clean aesthetics and user involvement.

Role

My Role & Deliverables

User Flow & Branching Logic: I architected the end-to-end decision tree, managing complex dependencies to ensure a flawless user journey.

  • Visual & UI Design: Created a minimal interface that emphasizes comfort, using a palette and typography consistent with the Big Ape style.
  • Prototyping: Developed high-fidelity interactive prototypes to test logic transitions and feedback loops.
  • Conversion Focus: Integrated progress indicators to minimize drop-off rates, aligning with Conversion Marketing best practices.

Outcome

Business Impact: Increased Conversions

The configurator was designed not just as a tool for the user, but as a process optimizer for the company. The main achievements include:

  • Automated Qualification: Every lead arrives fully profiled and consistent, eliminating the time previously spent on unqualified or incomplete inquiries.
  • Reduced Lead-to-Quote Time: By automating the initial selection, the internal team has significantly cut down the manual workload for estimates.
  • Anticipated Product Value: The interactive journey allows users to understand the product's features and value proposition before ever speaking with a consultant.
  • Efficiency for the Team: By filtering out non-qualified requests, the sales team can now dedicate 100% of their time to high-value-added activities and closing deals.
